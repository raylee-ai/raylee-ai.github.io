RELIGIOUS STUDIES

Ray Lee

Undergraduate in Religious Studies · Minzu University of China

I study religious epistemology and the questions that artificial intelligence raises for the study of religion.

My current reading follows a question in al-Ghazālī’s account of prophecy. If people find a teaching trustworthy and see its effects in practice, what grounds do they have for recognizing its teacher as a prophet? I am tracing that question through Deliverance from Error, The Revival of the Religious Sciences and modern scholarship.

I share reading notes and research in progress here. Each entry records its status and revision date.
