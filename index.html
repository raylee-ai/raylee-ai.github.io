Research & Writing

I’m Ray Lee, an undergraduate in Religious Studies at Minzu University of China. I study religious epistemology and the questions that artificial intelligence raises for the study of religion.

My reading of al-Ghazālī asks how a reader can distinguish a prophet from someone who inherits the same teaching. In my bibliography, I trace individual citations to see which links survive a change of research tools.
